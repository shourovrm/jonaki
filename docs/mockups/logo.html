Jonaki — logo and app icon

Four options. Each row shows the launcher icon in a circle and a squircle mask (phones use different shapes), at full size and at the 48 px size it has on a home screen, on a dark and a light wallpaper, the one-colour version Android 13+ uses for themed icons, and the logo with the name.

A · The lit j

A lowercase j whose dot is the firefly. The name's first letter and the app's one rule, only working things glow, in a single mark.

Reads well at 48 px. It's the most ownable of the four.

B · Firefly

The insect itself: two wings, a head and the glowing abdomen with its halo. This is the icon in the current build.

Friendly and literal; the wings get small at 48 px.

C · Step track

The Rail line track from the chat screen: two finished stations and the working one lit. It says "an agent that shows its steps".

Closest to what the app does; less tied to the name.

D · Light trail

The looping trail a firefly leaves in a long-exposure photo, ending in its light.

Most atmospheric; the thin trail is faint at small sizes.
