Jonaki — chat model settings

You add a service from a drop-down list, then its key, then any number of its models, found by search. Saved keys show their first three characters and dots. One model, marked with a star, is the default for new threads; the status strip's model menu lists every model you added. Two layouts: A keeps everything on the Settings page in cards that open, B gives each service its own page.

A · Cards on the Settings page

Flow
"Add service" opens a drop-down of the services you haven't added. Each added service is a card; tap it to open the key and its models.
Strength
Everything is on one page and one tap away.
Risk
With three or four services open, the page gets long.

B · A page per service

Flow
Settings shows one row per service with its masked key and model count. Tapping a row opens that service's page: key, models, default. Adding a service uses the same drop-down.
Strength
Settings stays short; each service's page has room for its own options later (base URL for Ollama, for example).
Risk
One more tap to reach the models.
