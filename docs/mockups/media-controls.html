Media controls in the message box

The Picture, Vector and Video chips stay where they are. The line under them, which today only reports the model and the price, becomes the place to change the model, the quality and the shape or length, with the price of the next send beside it.

Mockup at 360 dp. Model names and the $0.007, $0.08 and $0.12 figures are the ones the phone showed on 2026-10-10; the prices for High, 480p and 1080p are placeholders.

Working sample

Tap a chip, then a setting. The price follows the choice.

Option A: the details line becomes the settings row Recommended

Each value is a pill that opens its choices. The pills scroll sideways when a long name or a large font needs more room; the price is fixed at the right edge and never scrolls away.

Option B: one settings sheet per kind

The line stays text. A tap on it opens one sheet with every setting. It shows more at once and costs one more tap for each change.
